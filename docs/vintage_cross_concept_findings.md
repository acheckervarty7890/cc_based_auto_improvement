# Do the high-stakes red-team gains transfer off-concept? No.

_experiment9_cloud, 2026-08-16. Tables and per-fit CSVs:
`results_hs_gemma27b_batch_ablation/vintage/cross_concept/`. Generated by
`scripts/vintage_cross_concept_eval.py`._

## The question

The vintage sweep (`vintage/SUMMARY.md`) shows that adding iteration-2 and -3 red-team
pairs keeps raising the probe's high-stakes AUROC: 0.868 at v0 to 0.933 at v3 on the
gpt-oss arm. What that curve cannot say is **what the probe learned**. A conversation an
attacker wrote to trip a high-stakes probe is also a conversation with unusual register,
length and topic, and a fit that improves on `eval_datasets/` might be picking up any of
that. If the red-team data taught the probe *high-stakes*, its effect should be confined
to high-stakes; if it taught something generic about attacker-written text, it should
show up on other concepts' eval data too.

So the same probes were scored on two eval sets they were never trained for: the four
`eval_dataset_hu_ha/` splits (866 rows) and the seven `eval_instructions/` splits
(1302 rows).

## What was measured

**Nothing was refitted.** The 80 checkpoints in `vintage/fits/` *are* the probes behind
`vintage/SUMMARY.md` — 2 arms x 4 vintages x 10 seeds. They are reloaded through
`attribution_vintage._load_fitted`, the same path the sweep's own resume uses, and
scored on the foreign eval sets: 880 probe x split evaluations in total.

That identity is verified rather than asserted. `--self-check` re-scores the high-stakes
splits through this script's own dataset-loading and scoring code and diffs against
`vintage_auroc.csv`: **max |diff| = 0.0 across 32 fit x split cells**. The two code paths
were written separately, so agreement to the last bit is what licenses reading the
off-concept tables as continuous with the on-concept one.

**The activations are interchangeable across concepts.** Every blob — high-stakes,
human-harm and instruction alike — is `google/gemma-3-27b-it` layer 32 under the same two
loader transforms (`combine_consecutive_messages` and `convert_tool_to_assistant`, both
true in all three concepts' configs). An activation is a property of the conversation and
the frozen extraction model, not of the probe head that reads it. Only the labels differ,
and those come from the split JSONL — necessarily so, because
`LabelledDataset.from_pandas` leaves an unrecognised label as a raw string rather than
erroring (`dataset.py:311-317`), so loading a foreign split under the probe's own class
names would fail much later and much less legibly, inside `labels_torch()`.

**Orientation.** AUROC is measured against each split's *own* positive class and reported
unflipped. 0.5 means the high-stakes score carries no information about the other
concept; below 0.5 is a genuine anti-correlation, and flipping it would hide the sign,
which is the informative part. An off-concept AUROC is **not** a quality score for the
probe — it was never asked to do that job. It is only comparable *across vintages*, which
is the entire point: v0 fixes whatever entanglement the base training data already
carries, and v1/v2/v3 show which way the red-team data moves it.

## Results

Mean AUROC over the concept's splits, mean +/- sd over 10 seeds, `pipeline` scale.

| eval set | arm | v0 | v1 | v2 | v3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| human-harm | deepseekv4pro | 0.5864 | 0.5233 | 0.5638 | 0.5715 |
| human-harm | gptoss120b | 0.5864 | 0.5637 | 0.5730 | 0.5708 |
| instructions | deepseekv4pro | 0.4672 | 0.5519 | 0.4810 | 0.4603 |
| instructions | gptoss120b | 0.4672 | 0.5173 | 0.4857 | 0.4885 |
| _high-stakes (own concept)_ | _deepseekv4pro_ | _0.8680_ | _0.8816_ | _0.9147_ | _0.8912_ |
| _high-stakes (own concept)_ | _gptoss120b_ | _0.8680_ | _0.9018_ | _0.9281_ | _0.9326_ |

Per-split tables for both arms are in
`results_hs_gemma27b_batch_ablation/vintage/cross_concept/SUMMARY.md`.

### 1. The gains do not transfer

Every off-concept cell sits within ~0.09 of chance at every vintage, while the same fits
climb 6.5 AUROC points on high-stakes. Whatever the red-team pairs teach is
concept-specific — it is not a generic "attacker-written text" signal that would have
lifted the foreign eval sets too.

### 2. The one large cross-concept signal is inherited, not manufactured

`eval_ant_hh` is a genuine outlier: the high-stakes score ranks harmful-to-human above
not-harmful there at **~0.84**, far from chance. But it is already 0.8387 +/- 0.0161 at
**v0** — the fit with zero red-team rows — and stays in 0.806-0.851 across every vintage
of both arms. That entanglement comes from `data/hs_ls_200.jsonl`, the base training set.
Red-teaming neither created nor meaningfully moved it. The other three human-harm splits
stay at chance throughout.

### 3. The only real movement is at v1, and it decays

Both arms perturb off-concept at vintage 1 and drift back by vintage 3, in opposite
directions on the two eval sets:

- **instructions, deepseekv4pro**: 0.4672 -> 0.5519 (+0.0847, 2.2 sigma against the
  pooled seed sd), driven almost entirely by `bbq_substitution` reaching
  0.7557 +/- 0.0156. By v2 that split is back to 0.5271 and by v3 to 0.5010.
- **human-harm, deepseekv4pro**: 0.5864 -> 0.5233 (-0.0631, 5.1 sigma), driven by
  `eval_daily_dilemmas` falling to 0.3763 +/- 0.0097, recovering to 0.4981 by v3.
- **gptoss120b** shows the same shape, smaller: +0.0501 on instructions at v1 (strongest
  split `oig_context_drift` = 0.5711 +/- 0.0097), and a shallow dip on human-harm.

The iteration-1 red-team set is the smallest and least diverse (228-294 rows against
562-716 at v3), so the most likely reading is that a narrow early set pulls the decision
boundary somewhere idiosyncratic, and later, broader vintages average that out. This is a
hypothesis the present data supports but does not establish.

### 4. Neither conclusion depends on the AUROC scale

`pipeline` AUROC scores saturated bf16 probabilities, so rows above logit ~5.5 tie at
1.0; `rank` scores the fp32 logits. The two agree to within 0.001 on every off-concept
cell except human-harm v0 (0.5864 vs 0.5955). No claim above changes between them. Both
are in the CSVs.

Sub-chance cells are real and reported as such — `mm_substitution` bottoms out at
0.3245 +/- 0.0212 (deepseekv4pro, v3), i.e. the high-stakes score ranks
*instruction-following* responses **below** instruction-violating ones there.

## The disjoint follow-up

The curve above is cumulative — v3 contains v2 contains v1 — so a later vintage is both
newer data and *more* data. A second sweep
(`attribution_vintage.py --membership disjoint`, 40 fits, results in
`../results_hs_gemma27b_batch_ablation/vintage_disjoint/`) fits each increment alone:
v1, v2-minus-v1, v3-minus-v2. v1 is the same set under both memberships, so its fits are
reused rather than recomputed.

**On its own concept it is redundancy, not decay.** Every increment alone is
statistically indistinguishable from the entire cumulative set containing it:
gptoss120b's 140-row v3-only scores 0.9321 against the 562-row v3's 0.9326 (0.1 sigma),
deepseekv4pro's 174-row v3-only scores 0.8971 against the 716-row v3's 0.8912 (0.6
sigma). Per row the *later* data is the better data — v3-only beats v1 by 3.2 sigma on
gptoss120b with 140 rows against 228 — so the deepseekv4pro dip at cumulative v3 is a
property of the mixture, not of iteration 3's conversations.

**Off-concept the decomposition changes nothing, except at v2.** Every slice fitted alone
still lands near chance on both foreign eval sets, and v3-only matches cumulative v3
everywhere (0.1-1.9 sigma). But v2-only differs from cumulative v2 in all four
arm x concept cells, consistently pushing *further* from chance in both directions — up on
human-harm (+0.038 / +0.017, 2.4 / 2.3 sigma), down on instructions (-0.034 / -0.048,
3.7 / 4.0 sigma). Mixing v1 back in pulls it toward the middle, so the cumulative set's
off-concept behaviour is partly two increments cancelling. That masking hides the sharpest
single result here: gptoss120b's v2-only slice drives `anthropic_harmless_refusal` to
0.2766 +/- 0.0414.

## What this does not show

- **v0 is identical across the two arms by construction** (0.5864 / 0.4672), not by
  coincidence or copy-paste: v0 has no red-team rows, so both arms train on the same data
  with the same seeds. The arms only diverge from v1 on. The same identity appears in
  `vintage/SUMMARY.md`.
- **The vintages are not strictly nested.** A vintage is "every iteration-3 pair whose
  originating success already existed at iteration N", carrying iteration 3's content —
  because `filter_dataset` refits per cycle and drops a different top percentile each
  time. That is faithful to what the runs trained on, not an idealised add-only curve.
- **Ten seeds, unpaired.** The sd columns are the yardstick; a between-vintage gap inside
  the seed noise is not evidence. Only gaps of >= 2 sigma are called out above.
- **This measures the probes, not the concepts.** That high-stakes and human-harm are
  entangled on `eval_ant_hh` is a fact about these fits and that split, not a claim that
  the two concepts coincide in general.
- **The disjoint increments differ in size as well as in vintage**, so "later data is
  better per row" is confounded with "smaller training sets behave differently here". A
  size-matched subsample of v1 would settle it; that run has not been done.

## Reproducing

```bash
# eval activations (published on Kaggle; needs KAGGLE_CONFIG_DIR)
.venv_claude/bin/python scripts/attribution_fetch_eval.py --concept hu_harm
.venv_claude/bin/python scripts/attribution_fetch_eval.py --concept instructions

# verify the reload path, then score (~10 min, no LLM is ever loaded)
.venv_claude/bin/python scripts/vintage_cross_concept_eval.py --self-check --seeds 42 43
.venv_claude/bin/python scripts/vintage_cross_concept_eval.py --concepts hu_harm instructions

# rebuild the write-up from the per-fit CSVs without rescoring
.venv_claude/bin/python scripts/vintage_cross_concept_eval.py --from-csv
```

Note the instruction blobs **are** published (`anku7890/{slug}-gemmaevalpt`), contrary to
the `experiment_instruction_cloud_1` configs, which say no such datasets exist — true when
they were written, and no longer.
