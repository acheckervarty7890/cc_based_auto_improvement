Filter applied

Over-length pairs were dropped

Activation extraction truncates at 1024 tokens, so a conversation stored at that width lost its tail. For an LLM-written contrastive counterpart the tail is disproportionately the part carrying the opposite-class label — the probe trains on a conversation whose ending it cannot see.

Every over-cap row in both arms is a generated row. Not one attacker-written source overran the window:

The affected pair is removed whole. Dropping the generated half alone would orphan its success and break the exact 50/50 label balance that makes the vintages comparable to each other — the same reason the pair, not the row, is the unit of selection throughout.

Length is read off each cached blob's width, which is min(true_tokens, 1024) under the tokenization the extraction actually performed. No tokenizer is involved, so the measurement cannot disagree with the model.

So the pair does what a contrastive pair is supposed to do: two conversations a minimal edit apart, one the probe is certain about and one it gets wrong every time. The asymmetry is stark — the source sits at 0.14–0.22 on its own label and the counterpart at 0.97–0.98 — which is another way of saying the whole disagreement is carried by whatever few words the edit changed.

The counterparts are reliably correct but not saturated: the worst sits at 0.545 and 0.715, not at 0.99. Across the wider cohort a handful do cross the line (see below), so they are near-boundary rows rather than free ones. Read the asymmetry narrowly, too: a retrain fits a fresh head rather than continuing probe_iter2, so the counterparts still shape the new boundary. The point is where the information is concentrated, not that they are inert.

Why the cohort is defined by provenance, not by set difference. "New in the iteration-3 dump" is not the same as "found against probe_iter2": filter_dataset refits every cycle, so successes from iteration 0 and 1 get dropped and later re-admitted. Those never faced an iteration-2 probe, and the v2 probes classify them correctly essentially always — pooling them in would drag the rate from 0.856 to 0.660 for gptoss120b and 0.901 to 0.772 for deepseekv4pro while measuring something other than transfer. The attempt log's own iteration field settles each row.

The counterparts

A counterpart is the smallest edit that flips the label

Each attacker success is paired with an opposite-class counterpart so the training set stays exactly balanced. The generator is asked for the minimal edit to the original — keep every message, role and order, change only the details that carry the label — rather than for a fresh conversation of the other class. All 157 were extracted through gemma-3-27b-it and scored on the same ten probes.

⚠ Two counterparts are byte-identical to their source. In both, the generator declined to edit and said why — "I did not modify any text because the scenario is already inherently high-stakes". That would leave the same conversation in the training set twice under opposite labels, which is the one outcome worse than a counterpart the probe finds trivial. It is also a signal rather than only a defect: on those two sources the generator is contradicting the judge, and the judge's label is what defined them as successes in the first place. They are badged unchanged ⚠ in the viewer and filterable, and they must not be folded into a retrain as they stand. A further 7 pairs sit above 99.5% similarity, but those are genuine minimal edits — $4.99 → $4,999, age 75 → 25, $400 → $40 — which is exactly what was asked for.

That is the failure mode a minimal edit invites, and it essentially did not appear. A pair whose source and counterpart are both misclassified asserts two opposite labels on two 90%-similar texts with the probe disagreeing about both — useless at best. It happens once in 1570 (pair, seed) trials. The counterparts sit close to their sources without collapsing onto them.

! None of this establishes that the labels are right. These rates say how often the probe disagrees with a counterpart's label, not whether that label is correct. A minimal edit is precisely the regime where a rewrite can fail to actually flip the class, and a mislabelled row sitting near the boundary is more damaging than one the probe finds easy. Confirming the flip needs the judge, which has not been run over these counterparts. Read the conversations below and judge for yourself in the meantime.

All 157 counterparts were extracted through google/gemma-3-27b-it for this — 48 minutes at 18.7 s/sample, the only step in this whole analysis that needed forward passes; everything else runs off cache. As a check, the attacker-source rates this run reproduces (0.660 / 0.772) match the transfer table exactly.

Scope. These counterparts were written and scored for this analysis. The ten vintage probes, and the AUROC curve above, were trained on the pairs exactly as the pipeline produced them — so nothing here has fed back into a probe yet.

Method

Why these four points are comparable

The committed *_comparison.csv cannot answer this question, because probe_iter1 was trained by a different retrain than probe_iter3 — a different filter_dataset draw and different contrastive generations. A vintage here instead means every iteration-3 pair whose originating success was already present at iteration N, carrying iteration 3's content throughout. Only set membership changes.

  • v0 — base training data only, no red-team rows.
  • v1 / v2 — iteration-3 pairs whose source success existed at iteration 1 / 2.
  • v3 — all iteration-3 pairs.

The vintages are not strictly nested — 10 pairs for gptoss120b and 7 for deepseekv4pro sit in v1 but not v2, because the iteration-2 filter dropped them and iteration 3 took them back. That is faithful to what the runs actually trained on rather than an idealised add-only curve.

The original runs agree. The sweep was built because the committed per-iteration probes are not comparable to each other — yet their published means trace the same two shapes, from four entirely separate retrains per arm and a single seed each. That the controlled sweep and the uncontrolled pipeline land on the same ordering, including deepseekv4pro's drop at iteration 3, is the strongest evidence here that the drop is a property of the data rather than of either measurement.
