Eval AUROC vs. red-team success rate

One tab per probe concept; within a tab, one panel per attacker arm.

The two sections share an x axis: red-team iteration N attacks the probe scored as iterN in the AUROC charts, and its successes train iter{N+1}. Success rate is over scored attempts — exact duplicates are never written to the log, and near-duplicate openers are rejected before the probe runs, so neither is counted.
